Privacy Policy

Box Rush, by Logic Lab 406 LLC. Effective October 8, 2026 (updated for ads).

We made Box Rush to be played, not to collect your data. This policy explains what the game stores and why.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Box Rush does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it does not create an account. The game shows ads from Google, which collects some information as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress and settings: your levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, depot progress and sound settings are saved on your device so you can keep playing.
  • Play statistics: the game keeps a private log on your device of things like which levels you started, won or lost, so the game can be improved. It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Error reports: if the game hits an error, a short technical note is saved on your device for the same reason. It stays on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells us only that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it.

Advertising

Box Rush shows ads through Google AdMob: optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward (like extra coins, a life or a free continue), and occasional short ads between levels. Buying Remove Ads turns off the ads between levels.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location, information about your device and how ads are shown and used, and crash and performance data. Google uses this as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: on iPhone, Box Rush asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time under Settings > Privacy choices in the game.

Children

Box Rush is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
