Support

Need help with Box Rush? Start with the answers below, or email us at support@logiclab406.org. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I get more lives?

Lives refill one every 30 minutes, up to 5. You can also refill them with coins: tap the hearts at the top of the screen.

I lost a level. Did I lose a life?

Every try uses a life, and winning gives it back. So a life is only used up when you lose or leave a level.

My purchase didn't arrive.

Close and reopen the game. If it still isn't there, open the Store and tap Restore Purchases. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of the purchase.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device. If you set up your new iPhone from a backup of your old one, your progress comes with it.

How do I turn off sounds or vibration?

Tap Settings at the top left. Music and sound have their own volume sliders, and vibration has its own switch.

What do the stars do?

Stars from winning levels fix up Pop's depot. Only your best result on each level counts, so replaying a level for 3 stars helps too.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
